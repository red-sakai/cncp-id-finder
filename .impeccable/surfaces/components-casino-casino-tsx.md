# Casino (Games app)

Scope: the Games app inside the iCisco tablet, full screen. Mode: Experience (the floor leads); seated game panels follow Operate rules.
Audience/job: CNCP members on phones walk the casino as Axie, sit at a slot machine or the Hold'em table, and play with practice coins (simulated) or real Ciscoins (real deal).
Constraints: real-deal outcomes are decided on the server and signed into the ledger; nothing secret in readable tables; reduced motion respected; works one-handed at 390px.
